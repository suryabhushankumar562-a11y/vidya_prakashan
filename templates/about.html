About Us

Our story, our mission, our commitment to knowledge

Est. 1990

Dedicated to the Spread of Knowledge

Vidya Prakashan Mandir has been a cornerstone of educational publishing in India for over three decades. Founded in 1990 in Delhi, we started with a small collection of school textbooks and have grown into a comprehensive publisher serving students from Class 1 through university level.

Our name — Vidya Prakashan — means "Publisher of Knowledge" in Sanskrit. Every book we publish is carefully crafted by subject experts, reviewed by educators, and designed to be accessible, accurate, and inspiring.
